OUR STORY

Built on Precision, Creativity & Reliability

Beam & Blossom was created with a simple goal — to deliver high-quality laser engraved and UV printed products that feel personal, professional and beautifully finished.

From small personalised gifts to large-scale commercial signage, every project is treated with the same level of care and attention to detail.

We proudly support local creators, businesses, event stylists, and individuals across Melbourne and Australia.

MATERIALS WE USE

Premium Australian-Sourced Timber Materials

All materials are sourced locally in Melbourne from Australian suppliers to ensure consistent quality, sustainability and precision laser performance.

Beech

European Beech Laser Plywood is designed and manufactured in Melbourne for laser cutting applications.

Frequently selected for business cards, event invitations and premium personalised products due to its clean cutting performance and high-quality natural veneer finish.

Contains formaldehyde-free glue adhesives.

Applications:

3D models, cake toppers, key tags, invitations, jewellery, business cards, bookmarks, plaques and signage.

Jarrah

A specialty laser-grade plywood product made in Melbourne using carefully selected NSW Jarrah veneers.

Proudly Australian sourced and manufactured with formaldehyde-free adhesives, ideal for engraving, cutting and etching.

Applications:

3D models, laser cutting, etching, engraving, signage, jewellery and more.

Eucalypt

Specialty laser plywood made in Melbourne using carefully selected Eucalypt veneers.

Ideal for precision laser cutting, engraving and commercial applications.

Applications:

Laser cutting, engraving, signage, puzzles, awards and architectural models.

Acrylic Wall Art

A premium laser-grade product made in Melbourne from high-quality acrylic.

Custom acrylic wall art combines detailed laser cutting with a polished finish for distinctive interior displays.

Applications:

Interior design, retail displays, signage, crafts and decorative products.

UV-Printed Displays

A premium laser-grade product made in Melbourne from high-quality acrylic.

Custom UV-printed acrylic and plywood displays bring business details and QR codes together in a clear, practical format.

Applications:

Interior design, retail displays, signage, crafts and decorative products.

Plywood & Acrylic Business Signage

A premium laser-grade product made in Melbourne from high-quality acrylic.

Custom acrylic business signs provide a durable, professional display for studios, shops and events.

Applications:

Interior design, retail displays, signage, crafts and decorative products.
